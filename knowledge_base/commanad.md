The complete structural chain of command, ministry-level division of responsibilities, and geographic/functional commands for the Indian Armed Forces are mapped out below based on the sources.

---

### **1. Apex Political Leadership & Ministry Level**

* **Supreme Political Authority & Executive Command**:
  * **Supreme Commander of the Indian Armed Forces**: President of India.
  * **Executive Political Authority**: Prime Minister of India and the Cabinet Committee on Security (CCS).
  * **Ministry of Defence (MoD)**: Headed by the Defence Minister (Raksha Mantri).

* **Department of Defence (DoD)**:
  * **Leadership**: Civilian Defence Secretary.
  * **Core Mandate & Responsibilities**: Assigned the statutory subject of "Defence of India and every part thereof" under the Allocation of Business (AoB) Rules 1961. Manages national defense policy, capital acquisitions/procurement, defense land, external civil-military coordination, and non-service defense organizations (e.g., IDSA, National Defence College).

* **Department of Military Affairs (DMA)**:
  * **Creation**: Established on January 1, 2020.
  * **Leadership**: Chief of Defence Staff (CDS) acting as *ex-officio* Secretary. The CDS also functions as the Principal Military Adviser to the Defence Minister on tri-services matters and permanent Chairman of the Chiefs of Staff Committee (COSC).
  * **Core Mandate & Responsibilities** (under Second Schedule to Government of India Allocation of Business Rules):
    * Direct jurisdiction over the three Armed Forces (Army, Navy, Air Force) and the Territorial Army.
    * Oversight of the Integrated Headquarters of MoD (Army HQ, Naval HQ, Air HQ, and Defence Staff HQ).
    * Non-capital, revenue procurement exclusive to the three Services.
    * Works, staffing, postings, promotions, and training relating to the Services.
    * Promoting tri-service jointness in procurement, staffing, training, and logistics.
    * Facilitation and restructuring of military commands into Integrated/Joint Theatre Commands.
    * Promoting indigenous defense equipment usage by the Services.

### **2. Integrated / Tri-Services Commands & Functional Agencies**

* **Headquarters Integrated Defence Staff (HQ IDS)**: Apex tri-services operational and strategic planning body.
* **Operational Unified Commands**:
  * **Andaman and Nicobar Command (ANC)**:
    * **Established**: 2001 (India's first and currently only fully operational geographical theatre command).
    * **Headquarters**: Sri Vijaya Puram (formerly Port Blair), Andaman & Nicobar Islands.
    * **Strategic Role**: Joint theatre oversight guarding eastern maritime approaches and critical choke points (Malacca Strait / Bay of Bengal).
  * **Strategic Forces Command (SFC)**:
    * **Established**: 2003.
    * **Headquarters**: New Delhi / Strategic Command Centre.
    * **Strategic Role**: Integrated functional command managing and operating India's land, air, and sea-based nuclear triad under the Nuclear Command Authority.

* **Specialized Tri-Services Operational Agencies (under HQ IDS)**:
  * **Defence Cyber Agency (DCyA / DCA)**: Formed 2019; manages cyberspace warfare operations and defensive cyber networks.
  * **Defence Space Agency (DSA)**: Formed 2019; **Headquarters**: Bengaluru, Karnataka. Manages military space warfare assets and satellite intelligence.
  * **Armed Forces Special Operations Division (AFSOD)**: Formed 2019; coordinates joint special forces operations across services.

* **Proposed Integrated Theatre Commands (Active Theaterisation Plan)**:
  * **Northern Theatre Command**: **Headquarters**: Lucknow; Commanded by an **Indian Army Officer** (4-star General); Focused on continental defense along the Himalayan border with China.
  * **Western Theatre Command**: **Headquarters**: Jaipur; Commanded by an **Indian Air Force Officer** (4-star Air Chief Marshal); Focused on air-land combat and deterrence along the Pakistan border.
  * **Maritime Theatre Command**: **Headquarters**: Thiruvananthapuram; Commanded by an **Indian Navy Officer** (4-star Admiral); Focused on Indian Ocean Region (IOR) maritime domain security.

### **3. Indian Army Commands (7 Commands: 6 Operational + 1 Training)**

* **Northern Command**:
  * **Headquarters**: Udhampur, Jammu & Kashmir.
  * **Regional Jurisdiction**: Jammu & Kashmir, Ladakh, Line of Control (LoC), and Western LAC.
* **Western Command**:
  * **Headquarters**: Chandimandir, Haryana.
  * **Regional Jurisdiction**: Indo-Pak international border across Punjab, Jammu plains, and North Rajasthan sectors.
* **South Western Command**:
  * **Headquarters**: Jaipur, Rajasthan.
  * **Regional Jurisdiction**: Desert and semi-desert sectors along the Rajasthan and northern Gujarat borders.
* **Southern Command**:
  * **Headquarters**: Pune, Maharashtra.
  * **Regional Jurisdiction**: Southern peninsula, Gujarat desert/coastal sector, and West-Central India.
* **Central Command**:
  * **Headquarters**: Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh.
  * **Regional Jurisdiction**: Central India, Uttarakhand Himalayas border, and inland support/logistics reserves.
* **Eastern Command**:
  * **Headquarters**: Fort William, Kolkata, West Bengal.
  * **Regional Jurisdiction**: Northeast India, Eastern Himalayas LAC (Sikkim & Arunachal Pradesh), and Myanmar border.
* **Army Training Command (ARTRAC)**:
  * **Headquarters**: Shimla, Himachal Pradesh.
  * **Jurisdiction**: Functional command overseeing army-wide operational doctrines, officer academies, and institutional training.

---

### **4. Indian Navy Commands (3 Operational/Functional Commands)**

* **Western Naval Command**:
  * **Headquarters**: Mumbai, Maharashtra.
  * **Assigned Fleet**: **Western Fleet**.
  * **Area**: Arabian Sea, Western seaboard, and Arabian Gulf approaches.
* **Eastern Naval Command**:
  * **Headquarters**: Visakhapatnam, Andhra Pradesh.
  * **Assigned Fleet**: **Eastern Fleet**.
  * **Area**: Bay of Bengal, Eastern seaboard, and Malacca Strait approaches.
* **Southern Naval Command**:
  * **Headquarters**: Kochi (Cochin), Kerala.
  * **Primary Mandate**: Functional training command and southern peninsular maritime approaches.

---

### **5. Indian Air Force Commands (7 Commands: 5 Operational + 2 Functional)**

* **Western Air Command**:
  * **Headquarters**: New Delhi.
* **Eastern Air Command**:
  * **Headquarters**: Shillong, Meghalaya.
* **Central Air Command**:
  * **Headquarters**: Prayagraj (Allahabad), Uttar Pradesh.
* **Southern Air Command**:
  * **Headquarters**: Thiruvananthapuram, Kerala.
* **South Western Air Command**:
  * **Headquarters**: Gandhinagar, Gujarat.
* **Training Command (Functional)**:
  * **Headquarters**: Bengaluru, Karnataka.
* **Maintenance Command (Functional)**:
  * **Headquarters**: Nagpur, Maharashtra.
