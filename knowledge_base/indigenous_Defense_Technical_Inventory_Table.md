### **Detailed Programmatic Breakdown & Analysis**

#### 1. Strategic Missile Program & Anti-Ballistic Missile Defense
* **Agni-V Intercontinental Ballistic Missile**: Developed by DRDO, the **Agni-V** is a road-mobile, canisterised, solid-fueled missile featuring **Multiple Independently Targetable Re-entry Vehicle (MIRV)** capabilities designed to carry 10 to 12 maneuverable nuclear warheads over an operational range of 5,500 to 8,000 km. Navigation is provided by a Ring Laser Gyroscope-based Inertial Navigation System (RLG-INS) with MINGS backup linked to NavIC and GPS satellites. In 2025, conventional bunker-buster and air-burst variants carrying 7,500 to 8,000 kg payloads were also initiated.
* **Agni & Prithvi Missile Families**: Includes the tactical SRBM **Prithvi-II** (250–350 km range), as well as the **Agni-I**, **Agni-II**, **Agni-III**, **Agni-IV**, and **Agni-P** (which features a rail-based mobile launcher designed for rapid deployment with reduced visibility).
* **Two-Tiered BMD Program**: India's ballistic missile defense shield consists of high-altitude exo-atmospheric interceptors (**Prithvi Air Defence / PAD** and **PDV / PDV Mk2**) and lower-altitude endo-atmospheric interceptors (**Advanced Air Defence / AAD**). The Phase-II BMD architecture expands this envelope using **AD-1** and **AD-2** interceptors capable of countering 5,000 km class IRBM and ICBM threats.

#### 2. Surface-to-Air & Layered Air Defense Systems
* **Akash Missile System**: Designed by DRDO and manufactured by BDL and BEL, Akash features an indigenous content level of **96%**. The system operates alongside the **Rajendra 3D PESA fire-control radar**. Upgraded variants include **Akash Prime** (tested at high-altitude in Ladakh) and **Akash-NG** (dual-pulse solid rocket motor, AESA seeker, 70–80 km range).
* **Project Kusha**: DRDO’s sovereign long-range surface-to-air missile project designed to establish an area-defense umbrella with variants covering 150 km, 250 km, and 350–400 km ranges.
* **Integrated Air Defence Weapon System (IADWS)**: An indigenous multi-layered air defense grid combining **QRSAM**, **VSHORADS**, and high-power laser **Directed Energy Weapons (DEW)**.

#### 3. Command, Control, Communications & Artificial Intelligence
* **Akashteer**: An AI-powered, automated mobile command and control system developed by BEL and DRDO. It fuses Army air defense radars and sensors into a unified air picture and links directly into the IAF's **Integrated Air Command and Control System (IACCS)**.
* **Battlefield Tactical Intelligence Systems**: Includes **Project SANJAY** (Battlefield Surveillance System fusing ground sensors, UAVs, and radars up to 200 km beyond borders), **TRINETRA** (algorithmic target analysis and automated threat assessment), and **SAMA** (real-time C2 decision support tool).
* **Space-Based Surveillance (SBS-III)**: A proliferated 52-satellite mesh constellation developed by ISRO and the Defence Space Agency (DSA) providing persistent multi-domain surveillance.
