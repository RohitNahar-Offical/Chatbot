### **Chronological Defence Policy & Strategic Milestone Timeline**

- **1958**: **Establishment of DRDO** - To achieve self-reliance in critical defence technologies and equip the armed forces with indigenously designed advanced systems. - Expanded from an initial 10 laboratories into a network of 41 laboratories and 5 DYSLs.
- **1965**: **Establishment of Institute for Defence Studies and Analyses (IDSA / MP-IDSA)** - To conduct advanced research in defence, security, and international relations. - Provides policy-relevant strategic insights to strengthen national security decision-making.
- **2002**: **Promulgation of First Defence Procurement Procedure (DPP 2002)** - To standardize the capital acquisition process and ensure timely procurement of military equipment. - Established a multi-stage procurement cycle and official procurement classification categories.
- **2006 (November)**: **Prithvi Air Defence (PAD) Exo-Atmospheric Interceptor Test** - To develop an indigenous anti-ballistic missile capability against high-altitude threats. - Made India the fourth country in the world to successfully develop an anti-ballistic missile system.
- **2007 (December)**: **Advanced Air Defence (AAD) Endo-Atmospheric Interceptor Test** - To provide lower-altitude interception capabilities for a two-tiered Ballistic Missile Defence (BMD) shield. - Successfully demonstrated endo-atmospheric target interception, completing early Phase-I flight trials.
- **2012 (April 19–24)**: **Maiden Flight Test of Agni-V ICBM** - To establish long-range strategic deterrence and close the strategic missile gap. - Demonstrated canisterized, solid-fueled intercontinental ballistic missile capability with an operational range exceeding 5,000 km.
- **2016**: **Promulgation of Defence Procurement Procedure 2016 (DPP 2016)** - To prioritize indigenous design and development through updated classification categories. - Introduced a 15-year Long-Term Integrated Perspective Plan (LTIPP), 5-year SCAP, 2-year roll-on AAP, and established the *Buy (Indian-IDDM)* category requiring a minimum 40% Indigenous Content (IC).
- **2018 (April)**: **Launch of Innovations for Defence Excellence (iDEX)** - To facilitate rapid development of indigenized and innovative technologies for defence and aerospace by engaging startups and MSMEs. - Created a formal co-creation ecosystem connecting tech entrepreneurs directly with the Armed Forces.
- **2018 (September 28)**: **Approval of Tri-Service Domain Agencies (DSA, DCA, AFSOD)** - To institutionalize joint operational capabilities across space, cyberspace, and special operations. - Formally established the Defence Space Agency (DSA), Defence Cyber Agency (DCA), and Armed Forces Special Operations Division (AFSOD).
- **2019 (December 24) / 2020 (January 1)**: **Creation of Department of Military Affairs (DMA) & Office of Chief of Defence Staff (CDS)** - To enhance jointness in procurement, operations, training, and staffing across the three Services. - Restructured the higher defence organization to drive tri-service integration and theaterisation.
- **2020 (January)**: **Completion of Phase-I Ballistic Missile Defence (BMD) Program** - To establish an operational missile shield protecting major strategic centers like New Delhi. - Completed system development and made the BMD shield ready for government installation approval.
- **2020 (August 14)**: **Launch of Srijan Defence Portal** - To display imported defence items on a unified portal so domestic industry, DPSUs, and startups can design and indigenize them. - Uploaded over 500 imported naval items to drive domestic industrial manufacturing.
- **2020 (September 30 / October 1)**: **Release & Implementation of Defence Acquisition Procedure 2020 (DAP 2020)** - To promote 'forcible indigenisation', simplify Indigenous Content (IC) calculations, introduce ICT procurement and leasing categories, and streamline capital planning. - Raised IC requirements by ~10% across categories (e.g., *Buy (Indian-IDDM)* raised to 50%), introduced a 10-year Integrated Capability Development Plan (ICDP), and added the *Innovation* and *Leasing* categories.
- **2020 (December 14)**: **Promulgation of Allocation of Business Rules for DMA** - To formally codify the administrative, procurement, and operational jurisdiction of the newly created Department of Military Affairs. - Integrated Service Headquarters into the formal policy-making structures of the MoD.
- **2022 (July 16–18)**: **Unveiling of 'SPRINT Challenges' under NIIO** - To accelerate the induction of niche, indigenously developed technological solutions in the Indian Navy. - Engaged private industry and startups to field sovereign naval technologies under the *Swavlamban* framework.
- **2024 (March 4) / FY 2024–25**: **Launch of ADITI Scheme (Acing Development of Innovative Technologies with iDEX)** - To foster R&D in high-end critical and strategic defence technologies for the Indian Armed Forces. - Allocated ₹750 crore over 3 years to develop ~30 strategic technologies, build a network of 10 partner incubators, and achieve a 20% productionization rate.
- **2024 (December 31) / 2025**: **Declaration of 2025 as the 'Year of Reforms' by MoD** - To roll out integrated theatre commands, accelerate cyber/space/AI domain capabilities, and overhaul acquisition procedures. - Initiated a comprehensive stakeholder review of DAP 2020, promulgated new procurement manuals, and accelerated joint doctrine implementation.
- **2025 (April 17 – September 16)**: **Promulgation of Tri-Service Joint Doctrines & Publications** - To establish unified operational principles for joint warfare across multi-domain environments. - Promulgated 8 foundational joint doctrines and primers, including:
  - *Joint Services Staff Duties Manual (Vols I & II)* (Apr 17)
  - *Joint Doctrine for Cyberspace Operations* & *Joint Doctrine for Amphibious Operations* (Aug 7)
  - *Joint Primer for Integrated Logistics* (Aug 9)
  - *Joint Doctrines for Airborne/Heliborne, Special Forces, and Multi Domain Operations* (Aug 27)
  - *Joint Military Space Doctrine* (Sep 16).
- **2025 (August 15)**: **Announcement of 'Mission Sudarshan Chakra'** - To create an integrated, multi-layered air and missile defence grid (*Rashtriya Suraksha Kavach*) protecting national airspace against drones, cruise missiles, and ballistic threats. - Target completion set for 2035 to provide nationwide air defense coverage.
- **2025 (October 23 / November 1)**: **Release of Defence Procurement Manual 2025 (DPM 2025)** - To simplify revenue procurement procedures and ensure uniformity across the three Services and MoD establishments. - Streamlined processes governing approximately ₹1 lakh crore in annual revenue procurements.
- **2025 (Late 2025)**: **Promulgation of DRDO Procurement Manual 2025 (PM-2025) & DPFM-2025** - To encourage private sector and MSME participation in defence R&D, streamline project management, and simplify Technology Transfer (ToT). - Aligned DRDO's 10-year R&D roadmap with national goals for *Vision 2047* and implemented the SAMAR quality certification framework.
- **2026 (February 12)**: **DAC Approval of Major Capital Acquisition Proposals** - To significantly boost the combat readiness and operational capabilities of the Armed Forces. - Accorded Acceptance of Necessity (AoN) for capital acquisition proposals worth ₹3.60 lakh crore.
- **2026 (FY 2026–27 Union Budget)**: **Record High Defence Budget Allocation** - To drive force modernization, capital acquisitions, domestic defence production, and ex-servicemen healthcare. - Allocated an all-time high of ₹7.85 lakh crore to the Ministry of Defence (a 15% increase over FY 2025–26 BE), including ₹12,100 crore for ECHS.
