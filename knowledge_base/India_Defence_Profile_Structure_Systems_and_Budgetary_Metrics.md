### 3. Financial & Budgetary Metrics

#### **A. Capital vs. Revenue Expenditure Breakdown (FY 2026–27 BE)**
* **Total Defence Allocation**: Earmarked an all-time high of **₹7,84,678 crore** (~$86 billion / ₹7.85 lakh crore) in Union Budget 2026–27, representing **14.7%** of Total Central Government Expenditure and **2.0%** of India's GDP.
* **Revenue Expenditure**: Allocated **₹5,53,669 crore** (**70.6%–71%** of total MoD allocation):
  * **Defence Services Revenue**: **₹3,65,479 crore** (62% of Defence Services budget).
  * **Pay and Allowances**: **₹2,07,737 crore** (57% of Defence Services revenue expenditure / 26.40% of total MoD budget).
  * **Defence Pensions**: **₹1,71,338.22 crore** (21.84% of total MoD budget) disbursed to 34+ lakh pensioners via SPARSH.
  * **Spectrum Charges**: **₹37,200 crore** (10% of Defence Services revenue expenditure) allocated under a new budget head to settle telecommunication dues.
* **Capital Outlay**: Allocated **₹2,31,010 crore** (**29.4%–29.5%** of total MoD budget):
  * **Defence Services Capital Expenditure**: **₹2,19,306.47 crore** (95% of total capital outlay).
  * **Capital Acquisition (Modernisation)**: **₹1,84,932 crore** to **₹1,85,467.1 crore** (~₹1.85 lakh crore), marking a **24%–24.6%** increase over FY 2025–26 BE.

#### **B. Domestic Procurement Allocations & Minimum Indigenous Content (IC)**
* **Domestic Industry Allocation**: **75%** of the Capital Acquisition/Modernisation budget—amounting to **₹1,39,100.3 crore** (₹1.39 lakh crore / ~$15 billion)—is earmarked for domestic procurement in FY 2026–27.
* **Domestic Share Statistics**: **65%** of defence equipment is manufactured domestically; domestic industry accounted for **92%** of all defence contracts awarded in FY 2024–25.
* **DAP 2020 Minimum Indigenous Content (IC) Thresholds**:
  * **Buy (Indian - IDDM)**: Minimum **50%** IC on cost basis.
  * **Buy (Indian)**: Minimum **50%** (if indigenous design) or **60%** (otherwise).
  * **Buy and Make (Indian)**: Minimum **50%** of the 'Make' portion alongside ToT.
  * **Buy (Global - Manufacture in India)**: Minimum **50%** of base contract.
  * **Buy (Global)**: **Nil** for foreign vendors; minimum **30%** for Indian vendors.

#### **C. Research & Development (R&D) Allocations**
* **DRDO Total Allocation**: Increased to **₹29,100.25 crore** in FY 2026–27 (up from ₹26,816.82 crore in FY 2025–26), of which **₹17,250.25 crore** is for capital expenditure (~7%–8% of total MoD capital outlay).
* **Overall Defence R&D Spending**: R&D expenditure accounts for **3.7%** of the total defence budget in 2026–27 BE (down from 4.7% in 2014–15). India's overall national R&D spend stands at **0.64% of GDP**.
* **Industry / Startup Earmarking**: **25%** of the defence R&D budget has been earmarked for industry, startups, and academia since 2022. Approximately **₹2,000 crore** was carved out of DRDO's budget for deep-tech initiatives.
* **Innovation Schemes & Grants**:
  * **ADITI Scheme**: Earmarked **₹750 crore** budgetary support (FY 2023–24 to FY 2025–26), offering grants up to **50%** of product development budgets capped at **₹25 crore** per applicant across 30 critical technologies.
  * **Technology Development Fund (TDF)**: Allocated **₹17.08 billion** (₹1,708 crore) in 2026–27 BE. 81 projects sanctioned under TDF involving **₹344 crore** in committed funding.
  * **iDEX & ADITI (FY 2025–26)**: **₹449.62 crore** allocated. iDEX SPARK grants offer up to **₹1.5 crore** per project, while iDEX Prime offers up to **₹10 crore**.

### 4. Indigenization & Defence Production Data

* **Domestic Production Growth**: Domestic defence production reached **₹1.27 lakh crore** in FY 2023–24 and **₹1.54 lakh crore** in FY 2024–25 (growing at an annualized rate of 8% between 2016–17 and 2024–25).
* **Defence Production & Export Targets**: The Ministry of Defence targets **₹3 lakh crore** in domestic defence production and **₹50,000 crore** in defence exports by 2028–29 / 2029.
* **Defence Export Figures**: Defence exports reached **$4.1 billion** (surging 62%) in the fiscal year ending March 2026.
* **Defence Industrial Base**: Comprises 16 Defence Public Sector Undertakings (DPSUs, which account for an average of 80% of domestic production), over 430 licensed private companies, and ~16,000 MSMEs. The private sector contributes **21%** to total defence production.
* **Positive Indigenisation Lists (PILs)**:
  * The DDP and DMA have issued **5 Positive Indigenisation Lists** covering over **5,500 items** (including 509 specific major equipment lines).
  * **Item Statistics**: Over **3,000 items** have been indigenised as of February 2025.
  * **Embargo Timelines**: Import embargos on the initial 100/101 listed items take effect progressively from December 2020 to 2024, with Long-Range Land Attack Cruise Missiles embargoed in December 2025.
  * **Indigenised Categories**: Includes 159 out of 175 Army ammunition variants (90.85%), 53 out of 57 Special Clothing & Mountaineering Equipment (SCME) items, artillery guns, assault rifles, corvettes, sonar systems, transport aircraft, light combat helicopters, and radars.
* **Policy Milestones & Systems Platforms**:
  * **SRIJAN Portal**: Import substitution portal listing 38,000+ items, with **14,000+ items** indigenised by domestic industry.
  * **Emergency Procurement VI Capital (2025)**: Concluded 29 schemes worth **₹7,074.24 crore** (19 domestic schemes worth ₹4,577.33 crore and 10 foreign schemes worth ₹2,496.90 crore).
  * **Foreign Direct Investment (FDI)**: Liberalised in September 2020 allowing up to **74%** FDI via automatic route and above 74% via government route; cumulative FDI in defence industries stands at **₹5,516.16 crore** since April 2000.
